Cloudflare tunnels are used because this server is self hosted, so Cloudflare collects some data as well.

Basically, what a Cloudflare tunnel is is it sends (encrypted with TLS) data from the internet to where the server is and back.

The website (uses Flarum) also stores cookies to keep you logged in, etc. Cloudflare can also store their own cookies.

Their privacy policy is here.

If you want to delete or export any of your data that's on the database, you can use the Personal Data section in your account settings page, or you can email me at personal@anirudhsevugan.me

Any of your data stored offsite (outside the server) will take a couple of days to be deleted even after it's deleted from the server. It's kept so in case of hard drive corruption, all posts and messages can still be restored from an earlier backup.
