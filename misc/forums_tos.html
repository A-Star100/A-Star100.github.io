Terms of Use

Apparently, it's recommended to have a ToS. So, here we... GO!


Stuff to keep in mind

Please be kind and treat other members the same way you would want to be treated.

Don't share anybody's personal info (including yours) and don't impersonate anybody.

Content (posts, messages, and any links) shared should be appropriate for all ages, and can't be spam or illegal and stuff.

Try not to enable too many email notifications, as the email setup is limited to a certain number of emails per day.

According to COPPA, you need to be 13+ to make an account (because your email address is required to sign up). Keep this in mind.

If a notification shows that the server is under too much load or can't accept your request, try not to use it too much until that stops.

If anything happens to your account or data, moderators can try to help, but please follow general security practices (don't reuse passwords).

Moderators can delete posts or suspend/delete accounts when needed, keep this in mind.

This server is not guaranteed to be available all the time. So if you're sharing links to a forum post, keep this in mind.

_____

If you break these rules too many times, your account can be limited.

If you find any issue with this ToS, you can email me at personal@anirudhsevugan.me
